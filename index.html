What the desk does at each turn of the wheel

The wheel is simple on paper. The hard part is the bookkeeping and the discipline. That's the part Arowana handles.

  1. Pick stocks you'd own

    Start from a short list of companies you'd be comfortable holding.

    Your Want-to-Own list filters every scanner.

  2. Sell a put

    Choose a strike and expiration that pay enough for the risk.

    The put scanner shows yield, cash needed, and earnings dates.

  3. Handle assignment

    If the stock drops below your strike, you own the shares.

    Cost basis updates automatically with every premium.

  4. Sell a call

    Pick a strike above what the shares really cost you.

    The call scanner only suggests strikes above your basis.

  5. Review the month

    See what you earned, what it cost, and what repeated.

    The income tracker and coach sum it up in one place.

Three rules the desk won't break

Every signal shows its rule

No mystery scores. If a number drives a suggestion, you can see exactly how it was calculated.

Vol rank = today's 21-day price volatility, ranked 0–100 against its own history

No made-up numbers

When real data isn't available, you'll see an empty state and the reason, never a placeholder that looks real.

Education, not stock picks

Arowana helps you track, check, and stick to your own plan. It doesn't tell you what to buy.
