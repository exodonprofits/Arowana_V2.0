📋 Entry Checklist

  • Impulsive pierce of Fast EMA (±0.6%)
  • Reclaim (long): close back above Fast EMA (and ideally Slow EMA)
  • Reject (short): close back below Fast EMA
  • Optional: bullish/bearish candle near reclaim/reject level

🛡 Risk & Management

  • Stop beyond wick low/high or ATR stop
  • Partial at mean/previous swing; trail with Fast EMA
  • Avoid counter-trend vs market regime
