📚 BB Snapback Playbook

🎯 Lower Band Setup (Long)

Price pierces lower band (σ ≥2), RSI ≤30, volume spike ≥1.5×. Look for pin bar or engulfing candle for entry confirmation.

📉 Upper Band Setup (Short)

Price pierces upper band (σ ≥2), RSI ≥70, volume spike ≥1.5×. Mean-reversion target is the mid BB (20-day MA).

🛑 Stop Placement

Stop just beyond the extreme wick. Exit if price closes further outside the band on the next candle — trend may be continuing.

📈 Target

Mid Bollinger Band (20 SMA) = 1R minimum. Opposite band = 2R. Scale out at mid-band, run remainder to structure.

⚠️ Risk note: BB snapbacks work best in ranging markets, not strong trends. Always check the higher timeframe trend before fading a band touch.
