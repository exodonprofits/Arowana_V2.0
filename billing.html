❓ Cancelling

When does access end?
At the end of the period you have already paid for. Nothing is cut off the moment you cancel.
Do I lose my trades?
No. Your journal stays on your account and remains usable on the Free plan. You can export everything to CSV at any time from your account page.
Is there a notice period?
None. Cancel from the billing portal in one click, no email or phone call needed.
Can I get a refund?
Within 30 days of a paid plan starting, yes — no questions asked. Email support@arowanaprofits.com.
