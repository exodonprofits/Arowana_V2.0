Important Disclosures

What this platform is, what it is not, and the risks of the strategy it supports. Please read before trading.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Not Financial Advice

Arowana Profits is an educational tool. Everything it produces — scan results, covered call and cash-secured put candidates, roll-coach choices, income and cost-basis figures, guardrail warnings, the daily brief and pattern findings — is information for your own analysis, not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and nothing is tailored to your circumstances, objectives or risk tolerance. Consult a qualified professional before acting.

What the Scanners and Coach Actually Do

The scanners apply fixed, published rules to your own holdings and watchlist and show what comes out, along with the rule behind each result and a score we calculate. A high score means a contract fit our rules well on the day it ran; it is not a prediction and not an endorsement. The roll coach sets out the choices available on a position you already hold — hold, close, roll, or take assignment — with the arithmetic for each. It does not tell you which to pick, and it does not know your tax position, your other accounts, or anything happening in your life.

Options Risk Disclosure

Options trading involves substantial risk and is not suitable for every investor. Selling options can produce losses far larger than the premium received, and selling a cash-secured put obliges you to buy shares at the strike price even if the stock has fallen well below it. Before trading options you must receive and review the Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options from your broker. The wheel strategy is not a low-risk strategy: it produces small, frequent gains and occasional large losses, and the tools on this platform do not change that.

No Guarantee of Results

Past performance does not indicate future results. Annualised yields shown by our tools assume a trade is repeated at the same rate for a year and that nothing goes against you; they are a way to compare trades, not a forecast of income. Backtests, scores and projections come from models with real limitations, and your actual results will differ.

Market Data: Delayed and Estimated

Stock prices and indicators come from end-of-day data, updated once each weekday evening. Option prices come from a free feed whose quotes are delayed and indicative — estimates rather than the live bid and ask you would trade against. Every candidate and coach figure is labelled with its data source and quote time. Always verify prices with your broker before placing an order. We make no representation about the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of third-party data.

Your Own Data, Your Own Numbers

Income, win rate, cost basis, tax figures and pattern findings are calculated from the trades you entered or imported. If your journal is incomplete or contains errors, those figures will be wrong in the same way. The tax snapshot is a rough check of your journal, not a tax document: real wash-sale rules run 30 days either side of a sale, apply across every account you and your spouse own including IRAs, and can be triggered by options. Your broker's 1099-B is the authoritative record, and a CPA should handle your filing.

AI-Generated Content

These features use a large language model: the AI Coach chat and Trade Plan Builder’s AI draft, Portfolio Advisor’s Ask Advisor chat and Analyze Stock profile, Explain in plain English on the Income tab, Argue both sides on Check a Trade, the Retirement Planner write-up, the Thesis Builder scenarios, and the AI Morning Brief, AI scorecard, chart analysis and Signal check. Any of them can produce inaccurate, incomplete or outdated statements. Treat them as a conversation partner, not a source of truth, and never act on them alone.

The AI Coach, Ask Advisor, Explain in plain English, Argue both sides, the Retirement Planner write-up and the Thesis Builder scenarios are limited to explaining. Every number in a reply must already appear in your own data or in what you typed; a reply containing any other number is rejected and not shown. An Analyze Stock profile may contain no figures at all; its ratings out of ten are the model’s opinion, and the figures beside it come from our market-data provider. The AI Morning Brief, AI scorecard, chart analysis and Signal check are not checked this way; treat their figures as unverified. None of these features recommends a trade, and Argue both sides sets out the case for and against without choosing one; the Thesis Builder sets out bear, base and bull cases without choosing one or giving a price target.

Everything else does not use a language model: the scanners, roll coach, income tracker, Check a Trade’s rule checks and its For / Against lists, the daily emails and pattern findings are arithmetic applied to your data under the rules shown alongside each result.
