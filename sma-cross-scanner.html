📚 How It Works
📡 Live Mode
Fetches daily candles from Finnhub (BYOK free tier). Computes real SMA(short) and SMA(long) from closing prices.
📈 Strength %

📅 Days Since Cross
How many trading days ago the crossover happened. ≤5 days = fresh signal (highlighted amber).
