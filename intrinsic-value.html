📖 Ben Graham Formula

V = EPS × (8.5 + 2g) × (4.4/Y)
Classic Graham formula adjusted for bond yields. Good for stable earnings businesses.

💰 Dividend Discount (Gordon)

V = D₁ / (r − g)
For dividend-paying stocks. Assumes dividends grow forever at a constant rate.
