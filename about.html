What we built

Arowana Profits is a suite of 20+ tools covering every stage of the trading process — from scanning and planning, to journaling and analysis. Our AI agents (Arowana Trader and Portfolio Advisor) are powered by Claude, Anthropic's AI model, integrated with live market data from Finnhub.

The platform is designed for options traders, swing traders, and long-term investors who want to think clearly about risk and value — not just chase momentum.

We believe the best traders are the most informed ones. That's why every tool on Arowana Profits shows its work: the formulas, the assumptions, the limitations.

Our principles

  • Honest about uncertainty — AI analysis is a starting point, not a verdict. We always remind users to do their own research.
  • No dark patterns — No fake urgency, no misleading signals, no "hot tips." We build tools that make you think, not tools that make you trade more.
  • Education over entertainment — We'd rather you understand one trade deeply than place ten trades blindly.
  • Free tier is genuinely useful — The free plan gives you real tools, not a crippled demo. We earn upgrades by delivering value, not by withholding basic functionality.
