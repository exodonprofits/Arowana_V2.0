💡 Before you dive in
Three rules that will save you years of pain
📈
Process over predictions
Nobody consistently predicts the market. Build a repeatable process — entry rules, exit rules, position sizing — and follow it every time.
⚖
Risk first, always
Before asking "how much can I make?" ask "how much can I lose?" Never risk more than 1-2% of your account on a single trade.
🕐
Time in market beats timing
For long-term investors, staying invested consistently beats trying to find the perfect entry. Compounding works best with patience.
